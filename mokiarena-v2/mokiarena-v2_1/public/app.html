🏆 How Scoring Works (Winning Conditions)

DEPOSITS are the #1 scoring action. Carry Warts to the deposit zone. Each deposit = major points.

ELIMINATIONS knock out opponents. Worth less than deposits but control the map.

WART DISTANCE = total distance carried with Warts. Bonus points on top.

WINS give team bonus but losing team still scores individually.

⚡ KEY INSIGHT: 1 deposit ≈ 3-4 eliminations in score value. This is why Strikers dominate despite lower win rates — they rush deposits while Bruisers fight.

💡 Optimal Training Strategy

For Strikers/Sprinters: Train SPEED (faster deposit runs) → FORTITUDE (survive while carrying) → skip STR/DEF.
